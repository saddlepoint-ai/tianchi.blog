This is the first placeholder post for `tianchi.blog`.

The plan is simple: keep a lightweight public notebook for projects, interests, learning notes, and occasional longer essays. The site is intentionally static, so every Markdown file can be reviewed locally, committed to GitHub, and published automatically.

## What I want this place to hold

- Project write-ups that explain the problem, the tradeoffs, and the final shape.
- Notes on ideas I am currently interested in.
- Personal essays and technical observations that are worth keeping outside chat logs.
- A living index of things I am building, reading, and testing.

Future posts can be added as Markdown files in `src/content/blog`.
